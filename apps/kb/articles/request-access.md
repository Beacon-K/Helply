## Symptoms

- "Access denied" or "You need permission to access this item".
- A shared folder, Teams channel, or SharePoint site is missing for you but visible to colleagues.
- You need to start using a new application for your job.

## Before you ask

1. **Refresh / sign out and in again.** Access granted recently can take up to an hour to appear, and sometimes needs a fresh sign-in.
2. **Check with the owner.** Many SharePoint sites and Teams have an owner in your department who can add you directly — it's often faster than IT.

## How to request access

Open a ticket and include:

- **Exactly what you need** — the folder path (e.g. `\\fileserver\Finance\Reports`), site link, app name, or mailbox address.
- **Level of access** — read-only, or edit / full access.
- **Why** — one line on the business reason (e.g. "covering month-end for Emma while she's on leave").
- **How long** — permanent, or until a date.
- **Your manager's approval** — name your manager; IT will confirm with them if they're not already on the ticket.

> **Why approval?** Access follows the "least privilege" principle: people get only what they need for their job. It protects confidential data (payroll, customer records) and is often required by auditors.

## Temporary access

If you're covering for someone, ask for access with an **end date**. IT will remove it automatically afterwards — this keeps permissions tidy and secure.

## I think I have access I shouldn't

Please tell IT — you won't get in trouble. Open a ticket describing what you can see.
