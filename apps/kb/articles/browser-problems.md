## Quick test: private window

Open a **private window** (`Ctrl + Shift + N` in Chrome and Edge, `Ctrl + Shift + P` in Firefox, `Cmd + Shift + N` in Safari) and try the site there.

- **Works in private** → the problem is your browser's saved data or an extension. Clear the cache (below) or disable extensions.
- **Still broken** → try a **different browser**. If it's broken everywhere, the site itself may be down or blocked — ask a colleague to check.

## Clear cache and cookies

1. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete` (Windows) in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. On Mac Safari: Safari → Settings → Privacy → **Manage Website Data**.
2. Choose time range **All time**.
3. Tick **Cookies** and **Cached images and files**. (You can leave browsing history and passwords unticked.)
4. Click **Clear data**, then close and reopen the browser.

> **Note:** Clearing cookies signs you out of websites — you'll need to sign in again.

## Stuck in a sign-in loop

- Clear cookies as above, then sign in again.
- Make sure the computer's **date and time** are correct (Settings → Time & language → set automatically).
- If you have several Microsoft/Google accounts, use a separate **browser profile** for work.

## Disable extensions

Chrome: menu → Extensions → **Manage extensions**; Edge: menu → **Extensions**. Turn them all off, test, then turn them back on one at a time to find the culprit. Remove extensions you don't recognize.

## Update the browser

Chrome/Edge: menu → Help → **About** — it updates automatically; click **Relaunch**.

## Open a ticket if

A work website or web app doesn't load in any browser. Include the website address, the exact error message (screenshot), and which browsers you tried. Error pages mentioning "blocked by policy" or a security filter mean IT may need to allow the site.
