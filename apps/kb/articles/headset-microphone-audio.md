## First: check mute (it's the most common cause!)

- The **mute button in the meeting app**.
- A **mute switch or button on the headset** cable or earcup.
- The computer's **volume** and mute key.

## Choose the right device

Computers often have several audio devices (laptop speakers, headset, monitor speakers, dock) and pick the wrong one.

1. **In the meeting app:** Teams → Settings → **Devices** (Speaker and Microphone); Zoom → Settings → **Audio**. Select your headset for both.
2. **In Windows:** click the speaker icon on the taskbar → the `>` arrow next to the volume slider → pick your output device. For the microphone: Settings → System → **Sound** → Input.
3. **On Mac:** System Settings → **Sound** → Output and Input.

Use the **test call** / **test speaker & microphone** option in Teams or Zoom to check before an important meeting.

## Others can't hear me

1. Check the microphone is selected (above) and not muted.
2. Watch the input level bar (Windows Settings → Sound → Input, or the app's audio settings) move when you talk.
3. Microphone permissions: Windows Settings → Privacy & security → **Microphone** — allow access for your apps. On Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Microphone**.
4. Headset boom mic: make sure it's **flipped down** — many headsets mute when the boom is raised.

## Echo or others hear themselves

Someone is using **speakers instead of a headset**, usually with the microphone too close. Ask them to use a headset, or lower their speaker volume.

## Bluetooth headset problems

- Bluetooth headsets have two modes. If sound is **muffled or robotic in calls**, select the headset's **"Hands-Free"** device for the microphone, or use the headset's USB dongle if it came with one.
- Disconnect the headset from your phone — it may be connected to the phone instead of the laptop.
- Remove and re-pair the headset (Settings → Bluetooth & devices).

## Still not working?

Open a ticket with the headset model, how it connects (USB, 3.5mm jack, Bluetooth, dongle), which app, and whether it works on another device.
