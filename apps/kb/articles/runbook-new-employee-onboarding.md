## Trigger

A ticket from HR or the hiring manager with: full name, job title, department, manager, start date, work location, and required systems/access. **Ask for at least 5 working days' notice.**

## Before day one

### Account and licensing

1. Create the user account (naming convention, e.g. `firstname.lastname`), set department, title, manager and phone in the directory.
2. Assign the correct **license** (e.g. Microsoft 365 plan) and add to **groups** based on the role template (department distribution list, Teams, SharePoint sites).
3. Grant access to shared mailboxes, shared drives, and line-of-business apps listed in the request — **only with manager approval** recorded in the ticket.
4. Enforce **MFA registration** at first sign-in (or prepare a Temporary Access Pass).

### Device

5. Pick a laptop from stock in the **asset register**; update its status to **In use**, set the **owner** and department in Helply Assets.
6. Enroll/prepare the device: latest OS updates, security software, disk encryption on, standard apps (Office, Teams, VPN client, browser), printer mappings.
7. Label the device with its **asset tag**; record serial number and warranty date.
8. Prepare accessories: charger, dock, headset, monitor(s) as per role.

## Day one

9. Hand over the device; have the user **set their own password** and register MFA in front of you.
10. Walk through: signing in, VPN, Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, where to find the **knowledge base**, and how to **submit a ticket** in Helply.
11. Security basics: lock screen, phishing reporting button, never share passwords.
12. Confirm all requested systems work; note anything outstanding in the ticket.

## Close the ticket

Resolution note: account name, device asset tag, licenses and groups assigned, any follow-ups. Link the asset to the user in Helply.
