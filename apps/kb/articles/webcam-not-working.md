## Quick checks

1. **Privacy shutter:** many laptops have a small slider over the camera, or a camera on/off key (look for a camera icon with a slash on the `F` keys).
2. **Close other apps that might be using the camera** — only one app can usually use it at a time. Quit Teams, Zoom, browser tabs with video, and the Camera app, then try again.
3. **Pick the right camera** in the app: in Teams → Settings → **Devices** → Camera; in Zoom → Settings → **Video**. With a dock or external webcam, the app may be using the wrong one.
4. **Restart the laptop.**

## Check permissions

### Windows 11

Settings → Privacy & security → **Camera** → make sure **Camera access** and **Let apps access your camera** are on, and your meeting app is allowed. For browser meetings, also allow **desktop apps** access.

### Mac

System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Camera** → switch on the app (Teams, Zoom, Chrome…). You may need to quit and reopen the app.

### In the browser

Click the 🔒 / camera icon in the address bar and set Camera to **Allow**, then reload the page.

## External USB webcam

Try a different USB port directly on the laptop (not a hub), and check the camera works in the built-in **Camera** app (Windows) or **Photo Booth** (Mac).

## Still not working?

Open a ticket saying which app you use, whether the camera works in the built-in Camera app, and any error message. If you have a meeting starting soon, join from your phone as a workaround.
