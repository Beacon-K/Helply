## Wired keyboard or mouse

1. Unplug and plug back in — try a **different USB port**, preferably directly on the laptop rather than a dock or hub.
2. Test the device on another computer if you can. Works there → problem is the laptop; doesn't work → the device is faulty.

## Wireless (USB receiver)

1. **Replace or charge the batteries** — this is the fix most of the time.
2. Check the **on/off switch** underneath the device.
3. Move the small **USB receiver** to a port closer to the device, or away from metal and other USB devices.

## Bluetooth

1. Make sure Bluetooth is on: Windows Settings → Bluetooth & devices; Mac System Settings → Bluetooth.
2. Turn the device off and on.
3. **Remove the device and pair it again**: select it → **Remove device**, then put the device in pairing mode (usually hold a button until a light flashes) and click **Add device**.

## Laptop touchpad not working

- Many laptops have a touchpad on/off key (often `Fn` + an `F` key with a touchpad icon).
- Windows: Settings → Bluetooth & devices → **Touchpad** → make sure it's **On**.

## Keyboard types the wrong characters

- Typing `@` gives `"` or letters are swapped (`z`/`y`) → the **keyboard language** changed. Press `Windows + Space` to switch between installed layouts, or click the language indicator (e.g. `ENG`) on the taskbar.
- Numbers on the number pad don't work → press **Num Lock**.
- Letters come out as numbers on a small laptop → **Num Lock** or `Fn Lock` is on.
- Everything is in capitals → **Caps Lock**.

## Spilled a drink on the keyboard?

Turn the laptop off immediately, unplug it, turn it upside down to drain, and **open a ticket with Urgency: High**. Don't try to turn it back on to "test" it — that's what causes permanent damage.

## Still not working?

Open a ticket with the device model, whether it's wired / wireless / Bluetooth, and what you've tried. IT keeps spare keyboards and mice — often the fastest fix is a swap.
