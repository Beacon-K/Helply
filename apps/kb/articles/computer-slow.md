## Quick fixes (try in order)

1. **Restart properly.** On Windows, "Shut down" with Fast Startup doesn't fully reset the system — use **Start → Power → Restart**. Many laptops go weeks without a real restart.
2. **Close what you're not using.** Browsers with 30+ tabs, Teams, and Excel with large files all use lots of memory.
3. **Let updates finish.** If Windows Update is installing in the background, the computer is slow until it's done — see "Windows updates: pending, stuck or failing".
4. **Plug in to power.** Many laptops slow themselves down on battery. On Windows 11, check Settings → System → Power & battery → Power mode → **Best performance** (while plugged in).
5. **Free up disk space.** Below about 10% free space, everything slows down — see "Low disk space: how to free up room".

## Find out what's using the resources

### Windows

1. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Esc` to open **Task Manager**.
2. Click the **CPU** or **Memory** column to sort.
3. If one app is using a lot and you don't need it, select it and click **End task**. Don't end processes you don't recognize — note their name for IT instead.

### Mac

1. Open **Activity Monitor** (Applications → Utilities).
2. Sort by **% CPU** or **Memory**.
3. Quit apps you don't need (`Cmd + Q`), or force quit a stuck one with `Cmd + Option + Esc`.

## Reduce startup apps (Windows)

Task Manager → **Startup apps** tab → right-click apps you don't need at login (e.g. game launchers, music apps) → **Disable**. Leave security software, VPN and company tools alone.

## Signs it needs IT

- The computer is **consistently** slow even right after a restart with nothing open.
- Fans are loud all the time, or the laptop is very hot.
- Strange pop-ups or new toolbars appeared → see "Fake virus warnings, pop-ups and tech-support scams".
- The laptop is more than 4–5 years old and struggles with everyday apps — it may be due for replacement (IT can check the asset register).

## When opening a ticket, include

- When the slowness started and whether it's constant or at certain times.
- A screenshot of Task Manager / Activity Monitor sorted by CPU and by Memory.
- Your asset tag (sticker on the laptop), if you have it.
