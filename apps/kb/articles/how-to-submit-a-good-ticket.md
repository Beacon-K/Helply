## Before you open a ticket

1. **Search this knowledge base.** Most everyday problems (printer offline, password reset, VPN) have a step-by-step fix here.
2. **Restart the device or app.** A restart fixes a surprising number of issues. Save your work first.
3. **Check if it's just you.** Ask a colleague nearby — if everyone is affected, say so in the ticket. It changes the priority.

## What to write

A good ticket answers five questions:

- **What** is happening? Describe what you see, not what you think the cause is. "Outlook shows Disconnected in the bottom bar" beats "email is broken".
- **When** did it start? Did anything change just before (new update, new password, moved desks)?
- **Who** is affected? Just you, your team, or the whole office?
- **What have you tried?** List the steps from the knowledge base you already followed.
- **Exact error message.** Copy the text, or attach a screenshot (`Windows + Shift + S` on Windows, `Cmd + Shift + 4` on Mac).

> **Tip:** Leave the category as "Not sure — let Helply decide" if you're unsure. Helply reads your description and routes the ticket to the right team automatically.

## Choosing impact and urgency

Helply calculates the ticket priority (P1–P4) from these two answers, and priority sets how fast IT must respond.

### Impact — how much of the business is affected?

- **High:** the whole office, a whole team, or a customer-facing system (e.g. nobody can take payments).
- **Medium:** a few people, or one person who can't do their core job.
- **Low:** one person, and there is a workaround.

### Urgency — how quickly must it be fixed?

- **High:** work has stopped, a deadline is at risk today, or it's a security issue.
- **Medium:** work is slower or harder, but continuing.
- **Low:** an inconvenience, or a request that can wait a few days.

> **Please be honest with these ratings.** If every ticket is "High", real emergencies wait in line behind the rest.

## Security issues are always urgent

If you clicked a suspicious link, entered your password on a strange website, or lost a laptop or phone, open a ticket with **High / High** straight away and also contact IT directly. Minutes matter.
