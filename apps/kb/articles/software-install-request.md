## Why can't I install it myself?

Work computers don't give everyone administrator rights. This protects the company from **malware**, **unlicensed software** (which can lead to fines), and programs that break other tools. It's standard practice, not a lack of trust.

## Check the approved software first

If your company provides **Company Portal** (Windows: search the Start menu; Mac: Applications) or a similar self-service app store, open it — approved software there installs with one click and no ticket needed.

## Request software

Open a ticket with:

- **Software name and version**, and a link to the vendor's official website.
- **What you need it for** — one line of business reason.
- **Who else needs it** — maybe the whole team, which changes how IT licenses it.
- **Cost / license** — free, or does it need a paid license? Who will approve the cost (usually your manager)?
- **By when** you need it.

IT will check security, licensing and compatibility, then install it for you or add it to the Company Portal.

## Please don't

- Download software from unofficial sites, "cracked" versions, or browser pop-ups offering downloads.
- Use personal accounts for cloud services to store company data (e.g. personal Dropbox) without checking with IT.
- Install browser extensions that ask to "read and change all your data on all websites" unless approved.

> **Tip:** Free trials often convert to paid subscriptions and store company data outside approved systems. Check with IT before signing up for a work tool, even a free one.
