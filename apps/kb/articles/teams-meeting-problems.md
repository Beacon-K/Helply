## Can't join the meeting

1. **Join from the meeting link in the calendar invite**, not an old chat.
2. Make sure you're signed in with your **work account** (click your photo in Teams to check).
3. Try the **web version**: open the meeting link and choose **Continue on this browser**.
4. As a backup, **join from your phone** using the Teams/Zoom app or the dial-in number in the invite.

## Audio or video is choppy, frozen, or delayed

1. **Turn off your camera** — video uses the most bandwidth.
2. Close downloads, streaming and other video calls on the same network.
3. Move closer to the Wi-Fi access point or use a **network cable**.
4. Quit and reopen the meeting app.
5. See "Internet or network is slow" to test your connection.

For sound or microphone problems, see "No sound, or others can't hear me". For the camera, see "Webcam / camera not working".

## Screen sharing doesn't work

- **Mac:** allow screen recording — System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Screen & System Audio Recording** → enable Teams/Zoom, then quit and reopen the app.
- Share a **single window** instead of the whole screen if others see a black screen.
- Multiple monitors: make sure you picked the right screen in the share dialog.
- Sharing a video? Tick **Include computer sound** so others can hear it.

## Teams is slow or acting strange (clear the cache)

1. **Quit Teams completely** (right-click the Teams icon in the taskbar → **Quit**).
2. Windows (new Teams): press `Windows + R`, paste `%userprofile%\appdata\local\Packages\MSTeams_8wekyb3d8bbwe\LocalCache\Microsoft\MSTeams` and press Enter. Delete the contents of that folder.
3. Reopen Teams and sign in. Your chats and files are safe — they're stored in the cloud.

## Open a ticket if

Problems continue across several meetings. Include: the app and version, whether it happens on Wi-Fi and cable, and the time of an affected meeting (IT can look up call quality data).
