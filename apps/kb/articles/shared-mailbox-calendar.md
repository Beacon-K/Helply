## Open a shared mailbox

Shared mailboxes (e.g. `info@` or `support@`) are used by a team. Once IT has given you access:

- **Outlook for Windows / Mac:** it usually appears **automatically** in the folder list within about an hour. Restart Outlook if it doesn't.
- **Outlook on the web:** right-click **Folders** (or your name) → **Add shared folder or mailbox** → enter the mailbox address.

Don't have access yet? See "Request access to a folder, app, or shared mailbox".

## Send from a shared mailbox

1. In a new email, click **Options** → **From** to show the From field (only needs doing once).
2. Click **From** → choose the shared address (type it in the first time via **Other email address**).
3. Replies you send appear in the shared mailbox's Sent Items when set up by IT.

**Send As** means the recipient sees only the shared address. **Send on Behalf** shows "Your name on behalf of info@". Which one you get is decided when access is granted — say which you need in your request.

## Share your calendar

1. In Outlook, go to the **Calendar**.
2. Click **Share Calendar** (or right-click your calendar → **Sharing permissions**).
3. Add the person and choose a level:
   - **Can view when I'm busy** — only free/busy times.
   - **Can view titles and locations**.
   - **Can view all details**.
   - **Can edit** — they can create and change events.
   - **Delegate** — they can also accept meeting invitations for you (typical for assistants).
4. Click **Send**/**OK**. The other person gets an invitation to add your calendar.

## Open someone else's calendar

Calendar → **Add calendar** → **From directory / address book** → pick the person. You'll see what they have shared with you.

## Meeting room booking

Add the meeting room as a **location/room** in the invite (**Room Finder**). If a room doesn't appear or won't accept bookings, open a ticket with the room name.
