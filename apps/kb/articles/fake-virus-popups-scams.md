## Common scams

- A browser page suddenly goes full-screen with alarms: **"Your computer is infected! Call Microsoft at 1-800-…"**.
- Pop-up notifications in the corner of the screen from a website, saying you have viruses.
- A phone call from "Microsoft", "your internet provider" or "IT support" saying your computer has a problem and asking you to install software.

> **Real Microsoft, Apple, and IT staff will never cold-call you or show a phone number in a pop-up.** Never call the number, never install remote-access software for someone who contacted you.

## Close a scam pop-up safely

1. **Don't click anything inside the page** — including "Cancel" or "Close" buttons, which can be fake.
2. Press `Esc` to exit full-screen, or `Alt + F4` to close the browser (on Mac: `Cmd + Q`).
3. If the browser won't close: `Ctrl + Shift + Esc` → Task Manager → select the browser → **End task** (Mac: `Cmd + Option + Esc`).
4. When reopening the browser, **don't restore the previous session/tabs**.

## Stop website notification spam

If the pop-ups appear in the corner of the screen even when the browser is closed, a website has notification permission:

- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → **Notifications** → remove unknown sites.
- Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → **Notifications** → remove unknown sites.

## Real antivirus alerts

Your company's security software (e.g. **Windows Security / Microsoft Defender**) shows alerts in its own style and **never** asks you to call a number or pay. If you see a real alert saying a threat was found, don't dismiss it — open a ticket with a screenshot.

## If you already called, installed something, or let someone connect

1. **Disconnect from the internet** (turn off Wi-Fi / unplug the cable).
2. **Contact IT immediately** and open a ticket — category **Security**, **Urgency: High**.
3. If you gave card or bank details, call your bank.
4. Change your work password from a different device.

## Signs of ransomware — act immediately

Files suddenly renamed with strange extensions, can't open documents, or a message demanding payment to "decrypt your files": **unplug the network cable / turn off Wi-Fi, don't turn off the computer, and call IT right away.** Don't pay anything.
