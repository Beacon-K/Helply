## Recommended: the Outlook mobile app

The Outlook app is the easiest and most secure option for work email, calendar and contacts. Many companies **require** it.

1. Install **Microsoft Outlook** from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
2. Open it and tap **Add account**, enter your **work email**, then tap **Continue**.
3. Sign in with your work password and approve the **MFA** prompt.
4. If asked to install **Company Portal** / **Microsoft Authenticator** or to allow your company to protect data in the app, follow the prompts — this is normal for work apps.
5. Allow notifications if you want alerts for new email.

## Also useful on your phone

- **Microsoft Teams** — chat and meetings.
- **Microsoft Authenticator** — MFA approvals (see "MFA / Authenticator problems").
- **OneDrive** — access your work files.

## After a password change

The app will ask you to sign in again. If it doesn't, and email stops arriving, open the app → Settings → your account → **Reset account**, or remove and re-add the account. Leaving an old password saved on your phone can **lock your account**.

## What IT can and can't see

With app protection, IT manages **only the work data inside work apps** (e.g. can remove work email if the phone is lost). IT does **not** see your personal photos, messages, or browsing. Company policies may differ — ask IT if unsure.

## Lost your phone?

Report it straight away — see "Lost or stolen laptop or phone".

## Problems?

Open a ticket with your phone model, operating system version (Settings → General → About on iPhone; Settings → About phone on Android), and a screenshot of the error.
