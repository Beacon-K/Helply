## Symptoms

- A blue (sometimes black) screen saying **"Your device ran into a problem and needs to restart"**.
- The whole computer freezes — mouse and keyboard stop responding.
- The computer restarts by itself without warning.

## Right now

1. **Take a photo** of the blue screen with your phone, especially the **stop code** (e.g. `DRIVER_IRQL_NOT_LESS_OR_EQUAL`). This is the most useful clue for IT.
2. Let it restart. If it's frozen with no blue screen, hold the power button for 10 seconds to turn it off, then turn it on again.
3. Once back in Windows, **save your work often** until the problem is fixed.

## Common causes and fixes

- **Pending or bad updates** → install all updates and restart (Settings → Windows Update). See "Windows updates: pending, stuck or failing".
- **Docks and USB devices** → unplug everything except the charger. If crashes stop, reconnect devices one at a time to find the culprit.
- **Overheating** → keep vents clear; don't use the laptop on a bed or sofa for long periods. Loud fans plus crashes under load is a strong hint.
- **A newly installed program or driver** → think about what changed just before the crashes started and mention it in your ticket.

## Check when it happened (optional)

Windows keeps a history: search the Start menu for **Reliability Monitor** ("View reliability history"). Red ✖ icons show crashes by day — a screenshot helps IT.

## Open a ticket if

- It happens **more than once**.
- It happens with a specific app or device every time.

Include: photo of the stop code, how often it happens, what you were doing, and any recent changes (new software, updates, dock, dropped laptop).

> **Warning:** Frequent crashes can be an early sign of a failing drive. If you have important files only on this computer, mention it — IT may back them up first.
