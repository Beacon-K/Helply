## Clearing a paper jam

1. **Read the printer's screen** — most printers show where the jam is (tray, rear door, toner area) with an animation.
2. **Open the indicated door or tray.** If the printer has been printing a lot, parts near the toner/fuser can be **hot** — let it cool for a few minutes.
3. **Pull the paper out slowly with both hands**, in the direction the paper normally travels. Pulling fast or backwards tears it and leaves pieces inside.
4. Check for small **torn scraps** and remove them.
5. Close all doors firmly. The printer usually resumes automatically.

> **Warning:** Never use scissors, knives or pens to dig out paper, and don't force parts that don't move easily. Open a ticket instead.

## Preventing jams

- Fan the paper stack before loading it, and don't overfill the tray (stay below the max line).
- Use the right paper type and weight; don't reuse crumpled or damp paper.
- Adjust the tray guides so they touch the paper edges lightly.

## Faded, streaky or smudged prints

- **Faded or light** → toner/ink is low. Check the printer's screen for levels. Gently shaking a laser toner cartridge side to side can give a few more pages.
- **Vertical streaks or lines** → often a dirty or damaged toner drum. Many printers have a **cleaning** option in the menu (Maintenance / Tools).
- **Smudges that rub off** → the fuser may be failing, or the paper type setting is wrong.
- **Blank pages** → new toner cartridge with the **protective tape still on**, or out of toner.

## Replacing toner

Only replace cartridges if you've been shown how, and only use the supplies IT or the office manager provides. If supplies are missing, open a ticket (low urgency) with the printer name and the cartridge model shown on the printer's screen.

## Open a ticket if

Jams keep happening in the same place, quality doesn't improve after cleaning, or the printer shows a service error code. Include the printer name/location and a photo of the error or a sample printout.
