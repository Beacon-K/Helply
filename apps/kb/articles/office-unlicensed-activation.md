## Symptoms

- A red or yellow bar: **"Unlicensed Product"**, **"Product Deactivated"** or **"Sign in to activate Office"**.
- Word or Excel opens files in **read-only** mode and editing buttons are greyed out.

## Fixes (try in order)

1. **Check which account you're signed into.** Open Word → click your name at the top right. It must be your **work account** — a personal Microsoft account won't have the company's license.
2. **Sign out and back in:** File → **Account** → **Sign out**, close all Office apps, reopen Word and sign in with your work email.
3. **Restart the computer** and make sure you're connected to the internet (Office checks the license online regularly).
4. File → Account → **Update Options** → **Update Now** to install the latest version.

## Did you recently change jobs, roles, or get a new laptop?

Licenses are assigned to **user accounts**. If your account was recently changed, the license may not be assigned yet or may have been removed by mistake — open a ticket.

## Too many devices?

Microsoft 365 licenses allow Office on a limited number of devices per user. If you've installed Office on old computers you no longer use, you can sign out of them at `https://account.microsoft.com` / your Office portal → **Install apps** / **Devices**, or ask IT.

## Open a ticket if

The message remains after signing in with your work account. Include a screenshot of **File → Account** (it shows the product and license status) and your username.
