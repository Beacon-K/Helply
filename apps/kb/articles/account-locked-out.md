## Symptoms

- "Your account has been locked" or "The referenced account is currently locked out".
- "Your account is temporarily locked to prevent unauthorized use. Try again later."
- You're sure your password is right, but sign-in keeps failing.

## Why it happens

For security, your account locks after several wrong passwords in a row. The most common cause is **not you typing it wrong** — it's an old password saved on another device that keeps retrying in the background:

- Email on your phone or tablet still using the old password.
- A second computer or a remote desktop session still signed in.
- Office Wi-Fi saved on your phone with the old password.
- Saved credentials in Windows for a mapped drive or printer.

## What to do

1. **Wait 15–30 minutes.** Most lockouts clear by themselves (the exact time depends on your company's policy).
2. **Before trying again, turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data on your phone** so it stops retrying with the old password.
3. Sign in carefully once. Check **Caps Lock** and the keyboard language (the `ENG` indicator on the taskbar).
4. If it works, update the password on every other device straight away (see "How to reset a forgotten password").

> **Warning:** Don't keep trying. Each failed attempt restarts the lockout timer.

## If you didn't cause the failed sign-ins

If you haven't changed your password recently and don't have other devices signed in, someone else may be trying to get into your account. **Open a ticket with Urgency: High** and mention it — IT will check the sign-in logs.

## Still locked?

Open a ticket (a colleague can submit it for you) with:

- Your username / email.
- When it started and what you were doing.
- Which devices have your work account on them (laptop, phone, tablet).
