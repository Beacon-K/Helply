## "Plugged in, not charging"

1. **Check every connection**: charger into the wall, cable into the brick, plug into the laptop. Try a different wall socket.
2. **Look for a light** on the charger or the laptop's charging port. No light at all usually means a faulty charger.
3. **USB-C laptops**: use the charger that came with the laptop. Phone chargers and low-wattage adapters often can't charge a laptop (or charge it very slowly).
4. **Battery care settings**: many laptops deliberately stop charging at 80% to extend battery life. If it stops at exactly 80%, this is normal.
5. Restart the laptop with the charger connected.

## Battery drains too fast

- **Lower screen brightness** — the screen is the biggest battery user.
- Close apps you're not using, especially browsers with video, and Teams/Zoom when not in a meeting.
- Windows 11: Settings → System → Power & battery → turn on **Energy saver** / **Battery saver**, and check **Battery usage** to see which apps drain the most.
- Mac: System Settings → Battery → look at **Low Power Mode** and battery usage history.
- Batteries wear out: after about 3–4 years most laptops hold noticeably less charge. IT can check the battery health.

## ⚠️ Stop using the laptop if

- The case is **bulging**, the touchpad is lifting, or the laptop doesn't sit flat — this can be a **swollen battery**.
- It gets **very hot** while charging, or you smell burning.

Unplug it, don't charge it, keep it away from flammable things, and open a ticket with **Urgency: High**.

## Open a ticket if

The laptop still won't charge with a known-good charger, or battery life is under a couple of hours for normal work. Include your asset tag, the charger you're using, and a photo of any warning messages.
