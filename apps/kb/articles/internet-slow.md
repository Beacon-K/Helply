## Is it the internet, or the computer?

- If **everything** on the computer is slow (opening folders, typing), it's probably the computer — see "My computer is slow".
- If only **websites, email and cloud apps** are slow, it's likely the connection. Read on.

## Quick checks

1. **Test another device** in the same spot (e.g. your phone on the same Wi-Fi). Both slow → network problem. Only laptop slow → laptop problem.
2. **Run a speed test** in your browser and note the download, upload, and ping numbers — IT will ask for them.
3. **Close heavy tabs and apps.** Video streaming, large uploads (OneDrive syncing hundreds of files), and backup tools use a lot of bandwidth.
4. **Move closer to the access point** or plug in a network cable.
5. **Restart the laptop** — this clears stuck network drivers and background updates.

## Only one website or app is slow?

That service may be having problems. Check whether colleagues see the same thing, and whether the provider has a status page (e.g. search "Microsoft 365 status").

## Browser-specific slowness

If one browser is slow and another is fine, see "Browser problems: pages not loading, sign-in loops, slow".

## When to open a ticket

- Several people in the office are affected → open **one** ticket with **Impact: High**, and mention roughly when it started.
- Only you, after trying the steps above → open a ticket with your speed test results, where you're sitting, and whether you're on Wi-Fi or cable.
