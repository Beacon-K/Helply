## Nothing happens when I press the power button

1. **Check the power.** Is the charger firmly plugged into the laptop and the wall? Is there a light on the charger or laptop? Try a different wall socket.
2. **Charge it for 15–30 minutes** before trying again — a fully drained battery may not respond immediately.
3. **Hard reset:** unplug the charger (and any dock), then **hold the power button for 15–30 seconds**. Plug the charger back in and press power normally.
4. **Desktop PCs:** check the switch on the back of the power supply and the monitor's own power and cable.

## It turns on, but the screen stays black

1. Listen for fans and look for keyboard lights — the computer may be on with just the display off.
2. Press `Windows + Ctrl + Shift + B` (Windows) — this restarts the graphics driver and often brings the screen back.
3. Unplug external monitors and docks, then restart. The picture may be going to a screen that's off.
4. Adjust the brightness keys — it may be at zero.

## Stuck on the logo, spinning dots, or "Preparing automatic repair"

1. Wait 15 minutes — it might be finishing an update.
2. If nothing changes, hold the power button to turn off, then start again.
3. If Windows repeatedly fails to start, **don't reset or reinstall it yourself** — open a ticket. You could lose files that aren't backed up.

## Blue screen asking for a "BitLocker recovery key"

This is a security feature protecting your data, not a virus. Open a ticket (or ask a colleague to) with the **Key ID** shown on screen — IT can look up the recovery key. Never give the key to anyone who contacts you unprompted.

## Open a ticket if

- None of the above worked.
- The laptop was dropped, got wet, or smells burnt — **stop using it and unplug it**.

Include your asset tag, what lights or sounds you notice, and what happened just before it stopped working. If you have no other device, ask a colleague to submit the ticket for you.
