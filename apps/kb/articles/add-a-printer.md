## Before you start

- You need to be **in the office** (or on the VPN) to find the office printers.
- Printers usually have a **name or label** on the front — note it down, e.g. `PR-0001 2nd floor`.

## Windows 11

1. Open Settings → Bluetooth & devices → **Printers & scanners**.
2. Click **Add device** and wait for the list to load.
3. Select your printer from the list and click **Add device**. Windows installs the driver automatically.
4. Click the printer → **Print test page** to confirm.

### Printer not in the list?

1. Click **Add manually** (next to "The printer that I want isn't listed").
2. Choose **Add a printer using an IP address or hostname**, and enter the address IT provided or the one printed from the printer's **network configuration page** (usually in the printer's menu under Reports or Network).
3. Follow the prompts. If asked to choose a driver and you're not sure, stop and open a ticket.

## Mac

1. System Settings → **Printers & Scanners** → **Add Printer, Scanner, or Fax…**
2. Select the printer in the **Default** tab and click **Add**.
3. Not listed? Use the **IP** tab (globe icon), enter the printer's address, choose protocol **IPP** or **Line Printer Daemon – LPD**, and click **Add**.

## Set it as your default

Windows: select the printer → **Set as default** (turn off "Let Windows manage my default printer" first). Mac: choose the **Default printer** menu in Printers & Scanners.

## Need help?

Open a ticket with the printer name/location. If your computer asks for an **administrator password** to install a driver, don't try to work around it — IT will install it for you.
