## Symptoms

- A warning that your mailbox is **almost full** or **over its limit**.
- You can't send new messages, or people tell you their emails to you bounce.

## Check how full it is

- **Outlook for Windows:** File → **Info** → Mailbox Settings shows the space used.
- **Outlook on the web:** Settings (⚙️) → General → **Storage**.

## Free up space fast

1. **Empty Deleted Items** — deleted messages still count until this folder is emptied. Right-click **Deleted Items** → **Empty folder**.
2. **Empty Junk Email** the same way.
3. **Find large messages:** in the search box, type `size:>5MB` (or sort a folder by **Size**). Save important attachments to OneDrive, then delete the email.
4. **Clean up old newsletters and notifications:** search by sender and delete in bulk. The **Sweep** option in Outlook on the web can remove all messages from a sender at once.
5. Check **Sent Items** — it often holds large attachments you sent.

> **Tip:** Share files as OneDrive / SharePoint links instead of attachments. It saves space for you and every recipient, and everyone works on the same version.

## Archive instead of deleting

If your company has **Online Archive** turned on, you'll see an "Online Archive" folder in Outlook. Drag old folders into it — archived mail doesn't count against your main mailbox and is still searchable.

## Don't do this

- Don't export email to PST files on your laptop to save space unless IT tells you to. PST files aren't backed up and are easily lost or corrupted.

## Still full?

Open a ticket with a screenshot of your mailbox size. If your role genuinely needs more space, IT can review your mailbox limit or turn on archiving (manager approval may be needed).
