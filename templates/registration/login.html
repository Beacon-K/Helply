Tickets

From first message to a closed ticket.

  • Raised your wayEmployees describe the problem in plain words. IT can also open one for a colleague.
  • Sorted on arrivalA suggested category, then automatic triage and routing when Helply is sure.
  • Priority from impact and urgencyThe ITIL matrix, so P1 to P4 mean the same thing to everyone.
  • SLA clocks that pauseResponse and resolution deadlines, paused while you wait on someone.
  • Replies and private notesRequesters see every public reply. Internal notes stay with IT.
  • Hand over in one clickAny technician can change the category or the owner from a dropdown.
  • Every change on recordWho changed what, and when, on every ticket.
  • Closed with feedbackRequesters rate the fix; resolved tickets close themselves after a reminder.

Intelligence, built in

AI inside the workflow.
Not a chat box.

It works where the work happens: when a ticket arrives, when it needs an owner, and when someone asks “haven’t we solved this before?”

  • It sorts.A suggested category and a confidence level. When Helply is sure, it triages the ticket by itself.
  • It routes.The ticket goes to the technician with the lightest load on the team that owns that kind of problem.
  • It remembers.Related articles and similar past tickets appear beside the new one, so solved problems stay solved.

Every correction a person makes is measured, so the dashboard shows how often the suggestion was right.

Service levels

A promise you can see being kept.

Set a first-response and a resolution target for each priority in plain language, like 30m or 1d 2h. Helply pauses the clock while you wait on someone else and warns before a ticket is late.

  • P1 to P4 targets you controlChanged in seconds on one page.
  • At risk and breached tickets riseThey go to the top of every dashboard.

Cloud servers

See your servers before your users do.

Install a tiny agent with one command on Linux or Windows. Helply draws live CPU, memory, disk and network, and turns a real problem into a ticket with an owner and a clock.

  • Read-only by designThe agent only sends readings. It opens no ports and accepts no commands.
  • Where it runs, what it costsCountry, provider and renewal date sit beside the graphs.
  • Recovery noted for youWhen the server returns, the ticket says so.

Assets and renewals

Know what you own, and when it expires.

Laptops, screens, network gear, licences and cloud servers in one register, each with an owner, a location and a warranty or renewal date. The right people hear about it at 30, 14 and 7 days, then on the day.

  • Every device keeps its storyIncluding the tickets raised against it.
  • Repeat faults stand outReplacing a problem laptop becomes a decision, not a hunch.
